### Risk colours (MedGuard-specific)

Interaction severity has its own semantic tokens, so a high-risk warning never looks like a
normal brand button:

| Severity | Token | Utility classes | Light | Dark |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| High | `--destructive` | `text-destructive`, `bg-destructive/10` | `oklch(0.55 0.22 25)` | `oklch(0.70 0.18 25)` |
| Medium | `--warning` | `text-warning`, `bg-warning/10` | `oklch(0.50 0.11 65)` | `oklch(0.82 0.12 80)` |
| Low / OK | `--success` | `text-success`, `bg-success/10` | `oklch(0.48 0.10 155)` | `oklch(0.78 0.11 155)` |

Each has a matching `-foreground` for solid fills. Never show severity by colour alone: always
pair it with the word ("High risk") and an icon.

The skill's default dark-mode destructive (`oklch(0.65 0.22 25)` + white text) is only 3.4:1, so
dark mode uses a lighter red with near-black text instead.

## Typography — Manrope + JetBrains Mono

- **Display + body:** Manrope
- **Mono (times, doses, numbers):** JetBrains Mono

Wired via `next/font/google` in `frontend/src/app/layout.tsx`. CSS variables: `--font-sans`, `--font-mono`.

### Type scale (recommended)

| Role | Class | Use |
|---|---|---|
| Display | `text-5xl font-semibold tracking-tight` | Hero only, one per page |

## Tone and voice

### Words to use

Calm and warm: measured, patient, kind. Talk to the person as "you", in full sentences, and use
contractions. Say what to do and when ("Take it 30 minutes before breakfast"). Focus on their
routine, not on the product. Plain words over medical jargon; when a medical term is needed,
explain it once.

### Words to avoid

No urgency or alarm unless the situation genuinely is urgent (a high-risk interaction is; a
reminder isn't). No "utilize", "leverage", "seamless", "AI-powered" or exclamation marks. Never
tell someone to start, stop, skip or change a medicine or dose, and never diagnose. Point them to
their doctor or pharmacist instead. Don't call anything "safe" without the "check with your
pharmacist" context.

### Voice example

> Take Levothyroxine at 7:15, about 45 minutes before breakfast. Keep calcium at least 4 hours
> away. Show this routine to your pharmacist before you start.

## Usage dos and don'ts

**Do:**

- Use the shadcn CSS variables (`bg-background`, `text-foreground`, `bg-primary`) everywhere. Never hardcode hex.
- Keep the 4 px spacing grid (`gap-2`, `p-4`, `px-6`).
- Respect `prefers-color-scheme` by toggling the `.dark` class on `<html>` from the system setting.
- Use `font-mono tabular-nums` for dose times, gaps and strengths.
- Use the risk tokens (`destructive`, `warning`, `success`) only for risk and status, never decoration.
- Test every component in both light and dark mode before considering it done.

**Don't:**

- Hardcode colours (`#FF0000`, `rgb(...)`) in component files.
- Use `transition: all`; always name the property (`transition-colors`, `transition-transform`, …).
- Use the brand blue for warnings or the risk colours for brand accents.
- Override shadcn tokens per component. If a colour is wrong, fix it once in `globals.css`.
